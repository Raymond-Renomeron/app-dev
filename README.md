# My Favorite Series: Fallout

## About
**Fallout** is a *post-apocalyptic sci-fi* series on Prime Video, based on the video game franchise. It follows people from underground vaults and the wasteland above.

## Main Characters
- Lucy MacLean
- The Ghoul
- Maximus

## Top 3 Things I Love
1. The retro-futuristic 1950s style
2. The dark humor
3. The worldbuilding

## Why I Like It

The show mixes action, comedy, and mystery, and it stays true to the games.
